Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about organ donation.

Who can become an organ donor?

Almost anyone can become an organ donor regardless of age. Medical professionals evaluate every donor individually.

Will organ donation affect funeral arrangements?

No. Organ donation is performed respectfully and does not prevent an open-casket funeral.

Can one donor really save multiple lives?

Yes. One organ donor can save up to eight lives and improve more than seventy-five others through tissue donation.

Does my family have to pay for donation?

No. There is no cost to the donor's family for organ donation.
