ABOUT ORGAN DONATION

One Decision Can Save Many Lives

Organ donation is one of the greatest gifts a person can offer. It provides hope to patients suffering from life-threatening diseases and gives families a second chance at life.

By becoming an organ donor, one person can save up to eight lives and improve the lives of over seventy-five people through tissue donation.

Why Organ Donation Matters?

  • ✔ Gives patients a second chance at life.
  • ✔ Reduces the shortage of life-saving organs.
  • ✔ Inspires others to become donors.
  • ✔ Leaves a lasting legacy of kindness.
